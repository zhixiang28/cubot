Make Cubot…

Things you can do on Cubot herself
  • Tap: she hops happily. Keep holding: she purrs and says hello at 2 seconds.
  • Shake or spin her: she gets dizzy.
  • Hold the touch sensor and shake: opens settings on her screen. Tilt to move, tap to pick, hold 1 second to go back.
  • Powered off: hold the touch sensor for 3 seconds to turn her back on. Alarms still wake her.
